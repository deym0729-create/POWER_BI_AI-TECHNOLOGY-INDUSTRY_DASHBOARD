🤖 AI Industry Analysis & Business Impact Dashboard

Interactive Data Analytics Project | Microsoft Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The AI Industry Analysis & Business Impact Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze the growth, adoption, investment and business impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) across different industries and companies.

This dashboard provides meaningful insights into AI adoption rates, company performance, revenue, investment, productivity, job creation and future growth opportunities.

It helps users understand how Artificial Intelligence is transforming businesses and influencing different industry sectors.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze AI adoption across different industries and regions.
- Compare AI investment and revenue across companies.
- Understand AI technology trends and adoption stages.
- Evaluate the impact of AI on productivity and business growth.
- Analyze job creation, job displacement and cybersecurity incidents.
- Identify future investment and growth opportunities.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

Tool| Purpose

DAX| KPI calculations and measures

📊 Dashboard Pages & Features

1. AI Industry Overview

This page provides an overall view of AI industry performance.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

- Total AI Investment
- Average AI Adoption
- Total Records
- Total Companies

Charts & Visualizations:

- Companies by Region – Shows the distribution of companies across different regions.
- AI Adoption Trend – Displays changes in AI adoption over time.
- AI Adoption by Company – Compares adoption stages across companies.
- AI Investment by Industry – Shows investment distribution across different industries.

Filters: Region, Industry, Date and Country.

2. Company & Revenue Analysis

This page focuses on company performance, revenue generation and investment.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

- Total Companies
- Total AI Investment
- Average ROI
- Total Recorded Revenue

Charts & Visualizations:

- Revenue by Industry – Compares revenue generated across industries.
- AI Investment by Company Size – Analyzes investment across enterprise, large and small companies.
- Revenue Trend – Shows revenue performance over time.
- Revenue by Region – Compares revenue distribution across regions.

Filters: Country, Industry, Company Size, Company Name and Date.

3. AI Adoption & Technology Trends

This page analyzes how companies are adopting AI technologies and using them in different business activities.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

- Average AI Adoption
- Average Productivity
- Total Use Cases
- Total Technologies

Charts & Visualizations:

- Productivity by Adoption Stage – Compares productivity across different AI adoption stages.
- AI Use Cases – Shows the distribution of AI applications.
- AI Adoption by Industry – Compares AI adoption across industry sectors.
- AI Adoption by Technology Category – Analyzes adoption across different AI technologies.

Filters: Date, Adoption Stage, Industry, AI Use Case and Technology Category.

4. Investment & Future Growth

This page focuses on AI investment, return on investment and its future business impact.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

- Total Jobs Created
- Average ROI
- Cybersecurity Incidents
- Total Jobs Displaced

Charts & Visualizations:

- Cybersecurity Incidents by Industry – Compares cybersecurity incidents across industries.
- Jobs Created vs. Jobs Displaced – Shows the employment impact of AI.
- AI Investment Trend – Analyzes investment changes over time.
- AI Investment vs. Estimated ROI – Examines the relationship between investment and expected returns.

Filters: Region, Country, Date and Industry.
